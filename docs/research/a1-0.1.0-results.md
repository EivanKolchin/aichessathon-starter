the subsequent 0.2.0 selective-search changes or 0.2.1 hybrid pilot.

All **44 games** completed without an A1 failure. PGNs, legal histories and frame positions
were reconstructed and checked against recorded results. This audits the records, not the
execution environment. The ten-pair score against A0 is 90%; the conservative exploratory
95% Hoeffding interval is **[47.1%, 100%]**. Do not turn this small sample into a precise Elo gain
or claim established superiority from its point estimate.

The three-repeat benchmark contains 87 paired trials across 29 positions. All exact perft
counts matched. Over the 20 ordinary development cases, the geometric mean speed-up of median
perft timings was **9.18×**. Search NPS had a descriptive **7.75×** ratio, but search ordering,
node accounting and caching differ; it is not an identical-tree measurement or an Elo estimate.
The nine special positions were correctness cases, excluded from timing aggregates.
[Raw benchmark report](results/a1-010-benchmark.json)

The first benchmark worker exited with code `0xFFFFFFFF` and empty stderr, with no established
cause. Its record was retained. Two subsequent workers completed, including this three-repeat
run. Successful reruns do not explain the earlier failure.

The compiled backend improved the measured development results and enables further experiments.
It has not demonstrated a win against the tested full-strength Stockfish configuration.
